How it works

Up and running in three easy steps

  1. 1

    Register your company

    Create your company account and add your first job site. Takes about two minutes — no setup, no IT person needed. You can add more managers and sites any time.

  2. 2

    Add your crew

    Send each worker a link. They tap it, pick a password, and they're in. Android users can install the app, everyone else just opens it in their browser.

  3. 3

    Watch it live

    Your workers press Start when they arrive on site. You see who's working, where, and for how long — straight away, from your phone or computer.

For managers

Every site and every worker, on one screen

No more phone calls to find out who turned up. Open your computer or phone and see what's happening on every job, right now.

See your crew live

Know exactly who's on the clock, which site they're at, and how long they've been there. It updates by the second.

  • Who's working right now
  • Today's and this week's hours at a glance
  • Hours split by job site

Run all your sites in one place

Handle every job from one screen. Add a site, put workers on it, and keep the full record after the job is finished.

  • Add as many job sites as you need
  • Move workers between sites in seconds
  • Finished jobs stay saved, nothing lost

Timesheets done for you

Every worker, every day, laid out clean. Tap any shift to see the details, then send the whole week to payroll in one click.

  • All workers and days on one sheet
  • See notes and materials for each shift
  • Export to CSV and send to payroll in seconds
For your crew

One tap to start. One tap to stop.

No training. No paperwork. Open the phone, press Start, and get to work.

  • One tap to start or stop a shift

    One press to clock in, one to clock out. The time is saved right away, so the hours are always right and nobody argues at the end of the week.

  • Live timer, notes and materials

    The clock runs while they work. They can quickly add what they did and what materials they used, with no paperwork at the end of the day.

  • Their hours, their history

    Their job site is already set when they open the app. They can see every shift they've ever worked, with hours and notes — all in their pocket.
